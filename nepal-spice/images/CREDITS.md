# Image credits (real photographs)

All 26 food photos are real photographs collected via image/reverse image search
for this non-commercial classroom demo. They belong to their original
authors/sites. For a public release, replace them with your own photos or
properly licensed ones.

Every file is normalized to **800×600 JPEG** (centre-cropped 4:3, EXIF rotation
applied). `node tests/audit.js` section 11 verifies this automatically.
