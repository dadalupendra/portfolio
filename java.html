🏔️ Basic Information

Official Details

  • Official Name: Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal (संघीय लोकतान्त्रिक गणतन्त्र नेपाल)
  • Capital: Kathmandu (काठमाडौं)
  • Government: Federal parliamentary republic
  • President: Ram Chandra Poudel
  • Prime Minister: KP Sharma Oli
  • Currency: Nepalese Rupee (NPR / रू)
  • Calling Code: +977
  • Internet TLD: .np
  • Driving Side: Left

Language & People

  • Official Language: Nepali (नेपाली)
  • Other Major Languages: Maithili, Bhojpuri, Tharu, Tamang, Newari, Magar, Gurung
  • Total Languages: 123 living languages
  • Literacy Rate: ~68%
  • Major Ethnic Groups: Chhetri, Brahman-Hill, Magar, Tharu, Tamang, Newar, Kami, Yadav, Rai
  • Religion: Hindu (81.3%), Buddhist (9%), Muslim (4.4%), Christian, Kirant
  • National Anthem: "Sayaun Thunga Phulka" (Made of Hundreds of Flowers)

🌍 Geography & Nature

🏔️

Himalayas (North)

Home to 8 of the world's 14 highest peaks over 8,000m including Sagarmatha (Mt. Everest 8,848.86m), Kanchenjunga (8,586m), Lhotse, Makalu, Cho Oyu, Dhaulagiri, Manaslu, and Annapurna.

⛰️

Hills (Middle)

The mid-hills region (700-4,000m) includes the Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, and Bandipur. Home to most of Nepal's population with terraced farming and ancient towns.

🌾

Terai (South)

The flat lowland plains bordering India. Rich agricultural land growing rice, wheat, and sugarcane. Home to Chitwan and Bardia National Parks with rhinos and tigers.

🗺️ Key Geographic Facts

  • Borders: China (Tibet) to the north, India to the south, east, and west
  • Highest Point: Mt. Everest (Sagarmatha) — 8,848.86m
  • Lowest Point: Kechana Kalan, Jhapa — 59m above sea level
  • Major Rivers: Koshi, Gandaki, Karnali, Bagmati, Rapti
  • Major Lakes: Rara Lake, Phewa Lake, Begnas Lake, Tilicho Lake
  • Climate Zones: 5 (tropical to arctic — all within 200km)
  • National Parks: 12 (Chitwan, Sagarmatha, Langtang, Bardia, etc.)
  • UNESCO Sites: 4 (2 cultural in Kathmandu Valley, Lumbini, Chitwan)

🕉️ Culture & Religion

🛕

Hinduism (81.3%)

Nepal is one of the world's most Hindu countries. Major temples include Pashupatinath (Lord Shiva), Muktinath, Janaki Mandir, Manakamana, and Budhanilkantha. The Kumari (Living Goddess) tradition is unique to Nepal.

☸️

Buddhism (9%)

Nepal is the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama (Lord Buddha) in Lumbini in 563 BCE. Major Buddhist sites: Boudhanath Stupa, Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple), and Namo Buddha. Tibetan Buddhism is strong in the north.

🎶 Arts & Traditions

  • Music: Madal (drum), sarangi, bansuri, dohori (duet folk singing)
  • Dance: Maruni, Lakhe, Deuda, Tamang Selo, Dhime
  • Architecture: Pagoda style (originated in Nepal!), Newar wood carvings
  • Clothing: Daura Suruwal (men), Gunyu Cholo (women), Dhaka topi (cap)
  • Handicrafts: Thangka paintings, singing bowls, pashmina, Dhaka fabric
  • Martial Art: Khukuri — the iconic curved Nepali knife
  • Script: Devanagari (देवनागरी) — shared with Hindi
  • Calendar: Bikram Sambat (BS) — ~57 years ahead of Gregorian

🎉 Major Festivals

🪔

Dashain (दशैं)

The biggest festival. 15 days celebrating the victory of good over evil (Goddess Durga). Families reunite, receive tika and jamara blessings. Kite flying, animal offerings, new clothes.

🏮

Tihar (तिहार)

5-day festival of lights. Day 1: Crow worship. Day 2: Dog worship. Day 3: Laxmi Puja (goddess of wealth) with oil lamps. Day 4: Govardhan Puja. Day 5: Bhai Tika (brothers & sisters).

🎨

Holi (होली)

Festival of colors celebrating spring. People throw colored powder and water at each other. Also called Fagu Purnima. Major celebrations in Kathmandu and Terai.

🐍

Nag Panchami

Worship of snake gods. Pictures of Nagas placed above doorways. Believed to protect from snakes and bring rain for good harvests.

🪷

Buddha Jayanti

Celebrates Buddha's birth, enlightenment, and death. Major celebrations at Lumbini and Boudhanath. Monks chant sutras, butter lamps are lit.

🏺

Chhath Puja

Sun worship festival mainly in Terai. Devotees fast and offer prayers to the setting and rising sun while standing in water. Extremely disciplined ritual.

🎭

Indra Jatra

Kathmandu's biggest street festival. The Kumari (Living Goddess) is paraded through the city on a chariot. Masked dances, pole-raising ceremony.

🌕

Teej (तीज)

Women's festival. Married women fast for their husbands' long life. Dancing and singing in red saris. Celebrates Goddess Parvati's union with Lord Shiva.

🎊

Lhosar

New Year celebrations for Tamang, Sherpa, and Gurung communities. Feasting, traditional dancing, and cultural performances.

🍛 Food & Cuisine

🍚

Dal Bhat (दालभात)

The national dish — steamed rice with lentil soup, vegetables (tarkari), pickles (achar), and meat curry. Eaten twice daily by most Nepalis. "Dal Bhat power, 24 hour!" is a popular saying among trekkers.

🥟

Momo (मो:मो:)

Steamed or fried dumplings filled with meat (buff/chicken) or vegetables. Served with spicy tomato achar. Originally from Tibet, now Nepal's favorite street food. Found everywhere from streets to restaurants.

🫓

Sel Roti (सेलरोटी)

Ring-shaped sweet rice bread, deep-fried. Traditional during Dashain and Tihar. Made from rice flour batter. Eaten with yogurt or meat curry. A festival essential.

🥘

Newari Cuisine

The richest food culture. Chatamari (Nepali pizza), Yomari (sweet steamed dumpling), Choila (spiced grilled meat), Bara (lentil pancake), Aila (rice liquor), Kwati (mixed bean soup).

🍽️ More Traditional Foods

Gundruk

Fermented leafy greens — Nepal's superfood, rich in probiotics

Dhido

Buckwheat/millet porridge — staple in hilly regions

Thukpa

Tibetan noodle soup — popular in cold mountain regions

Juju Dhau

"King of yogurt" from Bhaktapur — creamy clay pot yogurt

📜 Brief History

563 BCE
Siddhartha Gautama (Buddha) born in Lumbini, southern Nepal
~300 CE
Licchavi Dynasty — Golden age of art, brought Hinduism and Buddhism together
1200–1769
Malla Dynasty — Built Kathmandu's famous Durbar Squares, temples, and pagodas
1769
Prithvi Narayan Shah unified Nepal's small kingdoms into one nation
1846–1951
Rana Regime — Hereditary prime ministers ruled; Shah kings were figureheads
1953
Tenzing Norgay & Edmund Hillary reach the summit of Everest
1990
People's Movement — Democracy restored, constitutional monarchy
2008
Monarchy abolished — Nepal becomes a federal democratic republic
2015
New Constitution adopted — 7 provinces created; devastating earthquake (7.8 magnitude, ~9,000 died)

✈️ Tourism & Trekking

🏔️

Everest Base Camp Trek

14-day trek to 5,364m. Fly to Lukla, pass through Namche Bazaar, see Everest up close. One of the world's most iconic treks.

🌄

Annapurna Circuit

Classic 2-3 week trek through diverse landscapes. Cross Thorong La pass (5,416m). Rice paddies, forests, alpine desert, and mountain panoramas.

🏞️

Pokhara

The lake city. Phewa Lake, Sarangkot sunrise, paragliding, World Peace Pagoda. Gateway to Annapurna. Nepal's tourism capital after Kathmandu.

🛕

Kathmandu Valley

7 UNESCO World Heritage monument zones. Pashupatinath, Boudhanath, Swayambhunath, Patan Durbar Square, Bhaktapur Durbar Square. Over 2,500 years of history.

🦏

Chitwan National Park

UNESCO site. Jungle safaris to see one-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers, gharial crocodiles, over 500 bird species. Tharu cultural performances.

🕉️

Lumbini

Birthplace of Lord Buddha. UNESCO World Heritage Site. Maya Devi Temple, Ashoka Pillar, monastic zones with temples from 25+ countries. A place of world peace.

💡 Amazing Facts About Nepal

🚩 Unique Facts

  • Nepal's flag is the only non-rectangular national flag in the world — two stacked triangles
  • Nepal has never been colonized by any foreign power
  • The cow is the national animal and is sacred (not eaten)
  • Timezone is UTC+5:45 — one of the few 45-minute offsets in the world
  • Nepal has more festivals than days in a year
  • The Kumari (Living Goddess) tradition is unique to Nepal — a young girl worshipped as a deity

🌟 Records & Achievements

  • Deepest gorge: Kali Gandaki Gorge (5,571m deep)
  • Highest lake: Tilicho Lake (4,919m altitude)
  • Most Everest summits: Kami Rita Sherpa — 30 times (world record)
  • Gurkha soldiers — among the world's most feared and respected warriors
  • Birthplace of Buddha — Lumbini, 563 BCE
  • Pagoda architecture — originated in Nepal, spread to China and Japan

// Nepal.java — Complete Java class representing Nepal
public class Nepal {
    private final String officialName = "Federal Democratic Republic of Nepal";
    private final String capital = "Kathmandu";
    private final int population = 30_000_000;
    private final double area = 147_516.0; // km²
    private final List<Mountain> mountains = new ArrayList<>();
    private final List<Festival> festivals = new ArrayList<>();

    public Nepal() {
        mountains.add(new Mountain("Sagarmatha", 8848.86, 1953));
        mountains.add(new Mountain("Kanchenjunga", 8586, 1955));
        mountains.add(new Mountain("Lhotse", 8516, 1956));
        festivals.add(new Festival("Dashain", 15, Season.AUTUMN));
        festivals.add(new Festival("Tihar", 5, Season.AUTUMN));
    }

    public String getGreeting() {
        return "नमस्ते! Welcome to " + officialName;
    }

    public Mountain getHighestPeak() {
        return mountains.stream()
            .max(Comparator.comparing(Mountain::getHeight))
            .orElse(null);
    }
}

// Mountain.java — Represents a peak
record Mountain(String name, double height, int firstSummitYear) {
    public boolean isEightThousander() {
        return height >= 8000;
    }
}

// Festival.java
record Festival(String name, int durationDays, Season season) {}
